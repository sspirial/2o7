# 2o7: Grand Theft Matatu (GTA 3D Nairobi 2007)

> *Kila stage ina stori.* (Every stop has a story.)

A 3D open-world GTA-style action driving game set in Nairobi during 2007. Roam a 3D city, commandeer vehicles — from iconic custom Nairobi Matatus (*Nganyas*) to yellow cabs, muscle cars, and police cruisers — complete high-stakes missions, hit rooftop stunt ramps, and evade the law!

---

## 🎮 Controls

### On Foot
| Action | Key / Input |
|---|---|
| **Move** | `W` / `A` / `S` / `D` or Arrow Keys |
| **Sprint** | `Left Shift` |
| **Jump** | `Spacebar` |
| **Look / Aim** | Mouse (Left-Click to capture mouse, `M` or `Tab` to release) |
| **Enter Vehicle** | `F` or `E` (when near any car) |

### Driving Vehicles
| Action | Key / Input |
|---|---|
| **Accelerate / Reverse** | `W` (Gas), `S` (Brake / Reverse) |
| **Steer** | `A` (Left), `D` (Right) |
| **Handbrake Drift** | `Spacebar` (Slide sideways with tire smoke & screech!) |
| **Car Horn** | `H` (Two-tone horn) |
| **Radio Stations** | `R` (Cycle between Afrobeats 254, Nairobi Synthwave, Drill, Off) |
| **Exit Vehicle** | `F` or `E` |
| **Flip / Unstuck Car** | `Backspace` |
| **Quick Warp Test** | `1` (Player), `2` (Matatu) |

## 🚗 Featured Vehicles

1. **Matatu "The Flash" (Kenyan Nganya Minivan):**
   - High-capacity urban beast with graffiti styling, roof carrier, and throbbing sound system.
2. **Nairobi Taxi Cab:**
   - Classic black-and-yellow checkerboard sedan, agile and nimble.
3. **Nairobi Runner GT:**
   - Metallic muscle car with racing stripes, fast top-end speed and sharp drift handling.
4. **Police Interceptor:**
   - High-speed patrol cruiser equipped with dual red & blue emergency lightbar and pursuit siren.

---

## 🏙️ City & Gameplay Features

- **3D Nairobi Cityscape:**
  - 4-lane interconnected avenues, intersections, crosswalks, and sidewalks.
  - Commercial towers, residential blocks, street billboards (Safaricom, Tusker, Stage 7).
  - Traditional Kenyan roadside kiosks with corrugated iron roofs (*mabati*).
  - Stage 7 Matatu Terminus bus bay.
  - Stunt ramps scattered across avenues for high-flying airborne jumps!
- **GTA Heads-Up Display (HUD):**
  - Live minimap / radar showing roads, vehicle blips, destination markers, and compass direction.
  - Cash counter (`$`), Health bar, Armor bar, and Wanted Stars (⭐ to ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐).
  - Digital speedometer (`KM/H`) and vehicle badge.
  - Radio station banners and mission objective notifications.
- **Police & Wanted System:**
  - Hitting pedestrians or reckless actions trigger wanted stars.
  - Police cruisers chase the player with sirens blaring, attempting pit maneuvers!
- **Mission Markers:**
  - Glowing 3D pulsing beacons around the city:
    - **Mission 1: Matatu Rush** (Stage 7 Terminus)
    - **Mission 2: Checkpoint Blitz** (Downtown Plaza)
    - **Mission 3: Evade The Heat** (Industrial Alley)
