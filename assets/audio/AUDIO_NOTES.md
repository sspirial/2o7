# 2o7 Audio Direction & Specifications

## Specifications
- Format: OGG Vorbis
- Bitrate: 64–96 kbps
- Channels: Mono for SFX, Stereo for Music
- Loudness target: -16 LUFS
- Master stems must be archived

## 14 Music Tracks Planned
1. **Msafiri (Main Theme)** — Benga guitar + Genge beat (~100 BPM). Used in Title screen & Matatu rides.
2. **Stage ya Kwanza** — Jaunty, curious street acoustics for Prologue.
3. **Piga Kelele A/B/C** — 96 / 102 / 108 BPM loops for touting mini-game.
4. **Kiosk Hum** — Lo-fi, friendly market ambient.
5. **Kura Jingle** — Parody campaign jingle (fictional parties).
6. **Mtihani Focus** — Ambient study rhythm.
7. **Foleni** — Communal humming, light shaker percussion for polling day queue.
8. **Tally Night** — Minimal pulse tension.
9. **Ukimya** — Sparse wind & faint transistor radio (Act III silence).
10. **Hema** — Gentle acoustic strings & shakers (Camp/School).
11. **Mazungumzo** — Dialogue circle choir swelling.
12. **Harambee House** — Quiet collective exhale.
13. **Kumbukumbu** — Memorial acoustic reflection.
14. **Safari Inaendelea** — Full-band optimistic finish (Epilogue).

## Key SFX Needed for Prototype
- `matatu_engine.ogg` — Idling / accelerating engine
- `door_slide.ogg` — Matatu metal door slam / slide
- `sms_beep.ogg` — Classic period monochrome phone beep
- `coin_clink.ogg` — Matatu fare coins
- `radio_tuning.ogg` — Tuning between FM stations
